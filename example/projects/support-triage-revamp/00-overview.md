# Support Ticket Triage Revamp

## What this is

A short, focused push in September 2026 to fix how Briarwood handles a spike in support
tickets. Before this, every spike turned into an ad hoc scramble: someone would dig
through tickets from scratch, the team would argue about whether it was "a real incident,"
and whatever got learned stayed in that person's head.

## Background

Two ticket spikes in August, three weeks apart, both traced back to the same kind of
onboarding confusion. Both times the team re-derived the same root cause from zero,
because nothing from the first spike had been written down anywhere findable.

## Goal

Stop re-solving the same problem, and give the team a fast, consistent way to triage the
next spike instead of everyone guessing at the process.
