# Support Ticket Triage Revamp — Retro

## What worked

Writing down the reasoning behind each decision, not just the decision, turned out to
matter more than expected. When a smaller spike came up on 2026-09-13, Priya pulled up
this file, and the whole triage took fifteen minutes instead of the usual hour, since the
"why" was already sitting there instead of needing to be re-argued.

## What we'd do differently

Should have written the root-cause step down after the first August spike, not the
second. The second spike cost the team the same hour of confusion the first one did,
purely because nothing from the first one was written anywhere anyone would think to
check.

## What happened next

The process stopped being specific to this project, since it applies any time ticket
volume spikes, regardless of what's causing it. Pulled it out into
[reference/example-runbook.md](../../reference/example-runbook.md) so it's findable
without digging through a project that will otherwise sit untouched once this work is
done. The decisions above stay here as the reasoning; the runbook stays short and just
says what to do.
