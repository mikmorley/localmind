# Onboarding Flow Redesign — Decisions

A running log of decisions made on this project, most recent first. See
[00-overview.md](00-overview.md) for background.

## 2026-10-06 — Combine steps 2 and 3 into one screen

Decided in the weekly sync (see
[meetings/weekly-sync-2026-10-06.md](../../meetings/weekly-sync-2026-10-06.md)) to merge
the "team info" (step 2) and "profile setup" (step 3) screens into a single combined step.
Rationale: most of step 3's abandonment happens because users think they're done after
step 2 and don't realize another screen follows. Combining removes the false stopping
point rather than just shortening step 3 on its own.

Owner: Dana, prototype due 2026-10-09.

## 2026-10-05 — Prioritize profile-setup drop-off first

Decided to prioritize fixing the step-3 (profile setup) drop-off ahead of the
invite-teammates flow, based on the September onboarding survey showing 34% abandonment
at that step — the largest single drop-off point in the funnel. See
[daily-notes/2026-10-05.md](../../daily-notes/2026-10-05.md).
